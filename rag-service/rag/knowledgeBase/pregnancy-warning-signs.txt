Title: Pregnancy Warning Signs Requiring Urgent Care
Topic: pregnancy, emergency

Most pregnancies proceed without serious complications, but it's important to
recognize a handful of symptoms that should prompt contacting a provider right
away rather than waiting for a routine appointment.

Signs that generally warrant urgent medical attention during pregnancy:
- Vaginal bleeding, especially if heavy or accompanied by pain
- Severe or persistent abdominal pain
- Severe headache that doesn't improve, especially with vision changes
- Sudden swelling of the face, hands, or feet
- A significant, sustained drop in fetal movement compared to what's normal
  for that pregnancy
- Fluid leaking or gushing from the vagina before term
- Fever, chills, or feeling generally very unwell
- Difficulty breathing or chest pain
- Contractions before 37 weeks that are regular and increasing

Any one of these on its own doesn't necessarily mean something is seriously
wrong, but they're the kind of symptoms where checking in with a provider
promptly — by phone, urgent visit, or emergency care depending on severity —
is the safer choice rather than waiting.

This is general information, not a diagnosis. A treating obstetric provider
should always be the one to evaluate specific symptoms.
